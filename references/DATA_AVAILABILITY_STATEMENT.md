## 正文

All analysis code, frozen protocol, statistical analysis plan, endpoint estimand cards, and the
release-gate configuration for this study are available in the repository
`admet-screening` (version 0.9.0 / capability 4.0-dev). The frozen Stage 1 protocol is
`PREREG_STAGE1_PACKAGE.md` (SHA256 `3399cdf5e4465081d6e4d9f8a2f84500f2507e93d229473936bffb7a97da2cf6`),
registered prospectively as a public commitment at https://dpaste.com/9TRMMK4Z5
(created 2026-11-24T01:21:35+08:00; 365-day retention) with an independent RFC 3161
timestamp from freeTSA (serial `0x0A6E1091`, 2026-11-23T08:23:52 GMT; verification files in
`anchors/tsa/`). **This is an interim public commitment and cryptographic timestamp, not a
registry DOI**; a Zenodo deposit with restricted file access is being added when repository
access is restored, and the public commitment will be retained as the precedence proof.

The OneADMET Supporting Information (`jm6c00049_si_002.zip`, 35,054,958 bytes) was downloaded
from the publisher's Supporting Information page for
J. Med. Chem. 6c00049 (https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c00049). Its SHA256 is
`09d8eb35f541253c61f1ced6acd383f260a624986c924ef967382953c535f60e`; the contained
`oneADMET.csv` has SHA256 `c0860bf9c47806d9867f95b84bdfda8a62c189f891e1d951765fdd283d51034c`.
Owing to its size (1.2 GB uncompressed), this file is **not redistributed** with the code and
must be obtained from the publisher by eligible users; its hashes are recorded in
`assets/external/oneadmet/PROVENANCE.json` and verified by `adt env-audit --deep`.

TDC datasets are used under their respective academic licences and are not redistributed;
download instructions and per-asset hashes are in `DATA_MANIFEST.json` (17 assets, core mode
excludes the 1.2 GB file). The second, independent DILI cohort was derived from the FDA
Liver Toxicity Knowledge Base DILIrank 2.0
(https://www.fda.gov/science-research/liver-toxicity-knowledge-base-ltkb/drug-induced-liver-injury-rank-dilirank-20-dataset),
with names resolved to SMILES through the NCI Cactus resolver; compounds overlapping the TDC
DILI training/test sets by InChIKey were excluded. The resulting 60-compound cohort and its
provenance are in `out_ext/external/DILIrank2_independent_DILI.csv` and
`DILIrank2_provenance.json`; the frozen evaluation is `references/EXTERNAL_COHORT2_VALIDATION.md`.

`DATA_MANIFEST.json`, `DOCKER_DIGEST` (image `sha256:16d408f9…c16fb`, base image pinned by digest),
`requirements.lock`, and `tools/verify_all.py` provide a one-command reproducibility path.
All reported statistics are generated by scripts in `tools/` from the committed JSON tables;
no result was entered by hand.
